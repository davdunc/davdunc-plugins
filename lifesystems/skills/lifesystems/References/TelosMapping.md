# TelosMapping — Meadows Concepts Mapped to the TELOS Life OS

The bridge table this skill exists for: where each *Thinking in Systems* concept lives inside `~/.claude/PAI/USER/TELOS/`, and what it means to read a life through it. Canonical definitions stay in the SystemsThinking skill (`Foundation.md`, `LeveragePoints.md`, `Archetypes.md`) — this file only maps them onto the Life OS.

## Concept → File Map

| Meadows concept | Leverage rank | TELOS location | How to read it |
|-----------------|---------------|----------------|----------------|
| Paradigm (the belief the system arises from) | 2 | `BELIEFS.md` | The highest-leverage file in the Life OS. Every stuck goal eventually traces to a line here — or to a belief that should be here and isn't. |
| Goals of the system | 3 | `GOALS.md`, `MISSION.md` | Compare stated goals against revealed behavior (PROJECTS, time allocation). The gap is the diagnostic, not the aspiration. |
| Self-organization (capacity to evolve structure) | 4 | `LEARNED.md`, `WRONG.md`, `FRAMES.md`, `MODELS.md` | The system's ability to change itself. A growing WRONG.md is evidence of a self-organizing system; a static one, of a defended one. |
| Rules of the system | 5 | `STRATEGIES.md`, standing habits/commitments | Your self-legislation: what gets a yes, what a workday is, what money may be spent on. Rules beat willpower; audit them, not effort. |
| Information flows | 6 | `METRICS.md`, `STATE.md`, review cadence in `updates.md` | What the system can see about itself, and whether the signal reaches a decision point. Most stalled goals fail here first. |
| Reinforcing loops | 7 | visible across `PROJECTS.md` + `updates.md` history | Compounding engines (skill→opportunity→skill) and vicious spirals (fatigue→poor habits→fatigue). |
| Balancing loops | 8 | rest rules, budgets, stops in `STRATEGIES.md` | The self-corrections. Missing balancing loops show up as burnout and blown budgets. |
| Delays | 9 | expected-delay annotations on goals/metrics | Life stocks lag interventions by weeks–months. Write the expected delay down at decision time, or the delay window reads as failure. |
| Stock-and-flow structure | 10 | environment & defaults (location, tools, what's within reach) | Physical/structural arrangement — often easier to change than behavior within it. |
| Buffers | 11 | financial slack, calendar slack, energy reserves | Resilience storage. "Wasted" slack found by an optimization pass is usually load-bearing. |
| Parameters | 12 | schedules, amounts, rates throughout | Where most self-improvement effort goes; lowest leverage. Fine to tune — never confuse with structural change. |
| System memory / behavior over time | — | `updates.md`, `PROJECTS_STATUS.md`, `Backups/` | The BOT (behavior-over-time) graph of the life. Read first, always: "get the beat." |
| Purpose (revealed) | — | `PROJECTS.md` + real time allocation | Deduced from behavior, never from statements. |

## Life Stocks Inventory

Standard stocks to check in any mapping — each with typical inflows/outflows and delay scale:

| Stock | Typical inflows | Typical outflows | Response delay |
|-------|-----------------|------------------|----------------|
| Energy | sleep, exercise, meaning | overcommitment, conflict, poor health | days–weeks |
| Health | training, nutrition, rest | stress, neglect, age | months |
| Money | income streams | spending, obligations | weeks–months |
| Skills | deliberate practice | atrophy, field drift | months–years |
| Relationships | attention, reliability | neglect, breach of trust | months (build) / instant (breach) |
| Trust & reputation | kept promises, public contribution | overclaiming, absence | years (build) / days (drain) |
| Attention (per period) | fixed endowment | every open commitment | renewed daily; overdraft compounds |
| Options | savings, health, skills, network | lock-in, debt, burned bridges | slow both ways |

Attention is the commons of the Life OS — the shared resource every project draws on without pricing. Most personal tragedy-of-the-commons cases are attention cases.

## The Eight Traps — Life Recognition Signs and Ways Out

Structures in SystemsThinking `Archetypes.md`; this table is the life-domain overlay used by TrapScan.

| Trap | Recognition signs in TELOS | Canonical way out |
|------|---------------------------|-------------------|
| Policy resistance | Same fix appears in multiple update cycles, each time undone | Stop pushing harder; align the goals of all actors (including future-you and family) |
| Tragedy of the commons | CHALLENGES complains of overload while PROJECTS keeps growing | Make the commons visible and priced: attention budget, hard WIP limit |
| Drift to low performance | Goals quietly restated downward across revisions; "considering everything…" | Absolute standards or best-past benchmarks; never benchmark the recent past |
| Escalation | Metrics defined relative to someone else (peers, feeds, colleagues) | Refuse the race: unilateral de-escalation, or change reference group |
| Success to the successful | One project/skill/relationship absorbs all investment; others starve then "fail" | Diversification floors; scheduled rebalancing of attention |
| Shifting the burden | A relief behavior grows while the underlying capacity shrinks (caffeine↑ sleep↓) | Rebuild the system's own capability while tapering the intervenor — both, deliberately |
| Rule beating | Streaks/metrics intact while the purpose stalls; letter-not-spirit compliance | Redesign the rule so following its letter serves its purpose |
| Seeking the wrong goal | Proxy metric climbing, underlying stock flat (income↑ freedom↓) | Restate the goal as the actual desired stock; re-derive metrics from it |

## Delay Discipline

The single most common way life-system interventions die: evaluated inside their delay window.

- Flow changes are visible immediately (did the session happen?)
- Stock changes lag by the stock's response delay (table above)
- **Rule: every proposed intervention ships with a written expected delay, and is not judged before it.** Track flow compliance inside the window; judge stock movement only after it.

## Reading Order for a Full Analysis

1. `updates.md` — the beat (behavior over time)
2. `PROJECTS.md` + `PROJECTS_STATUS.md` — revealed purpose
3. `GOALS.md` / `MISSION.md` — stated purpose (now compare)
4. `CHALLENGES.md` / `PROBLEMS.md` — where the system reports pain
5. `STRATEGIES.md` — the rules layer
6. `METRICS.md` / `STATE.md` — the information layer
7. `BELIEFS.md` — the paradigm layer (read last, so the reading is informed by behavior, not colored by aspiration)
